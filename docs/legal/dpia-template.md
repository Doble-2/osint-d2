# Data Protection Impact Assessment (Template)

!!! warning "Draft"
    This is a starting point for legal review, not legal advice. Each operator is responsible for their own assessment, because the project does not process your data. Under the GDPR, the person deciding why and how data is processed is usually the controller.

Complete this before using OSINT-D2 on other people in a professional context.
