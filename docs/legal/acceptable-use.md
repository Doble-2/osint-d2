# Acceptable Use Policy

!!! warning "Draft"
    This is a draft for legal review, not legal advice. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer.

OSINT-D2 collects and infers information about people. You run it yourself, and you are responsible for how you use it.

## Allowed

Use OSINT-D2 only with a lawful basis and, where required, authorization. Examples:

- Checking your own digital footprint.
- Authorized security assessments.
- Journalism and research that follow the laws and ethical rules that apply to you.
- Investigating fraud or threats, within your legal authority.

## Not allowed

- Stalking, harassing, threatening, or exposing anyone.
- Making decisions about employment, housing, credit, insurance, or access to services based on its output.
- Inferring or recording someone's health, sexuality, religion, politics, or ethnicity without a specific legal basis.
- Targeting children.
- Preparing fraud, social engineering, or unauthorized access.
- Selling or publishing dossiers about people.
- Breaking the law or the terms of the services you query.

## Sensitive inferences

Personality, ideology, and attack-surface sections are off by default. Enable them only if you have a specific and lawful reason. They are speculative, so don't present them as fact.

## Your responsibilities

- Confirm findings before relying on them. Namesakes and model errors are common.
- Collect only what your purpose requires.
- Delete reports when you no longer need them. See [`osint-d2 purge`](../guides/cli-reference.md#purge).
- Respond to the rights requests of the people you investigate, where the law requires it.
